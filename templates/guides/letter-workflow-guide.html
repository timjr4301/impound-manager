STEP 1 Export Towbook & Import Into Impound Manager ▾

Done every morning. Syncs today's call list into IM so new vehicles appear and any vehicles that were released in Towbook get flagged.

Heather Tim · Brady

In Towbook

  • 1Log into Towbook. Go to your calls/reports section and export the current call list as a CSV file. Save it to your desktop or Downloads folder.

In Impound Manager

  • 2Click Daily Intake in the top navbar.
  • 3On the left panel labeled "Towbook Export" — drag your CSV file onto the drop zone, or click to browse and select it.
  • 4Wait a few seconds. A result badge will appear showing "X new · Y updated · Z skipped."
  • 5Check the top of the page for any sync warning banners. A yellow banner means one or more vehicles were in IM but absent from the CSV — they've been flagged as Possible Release and need verification before any letters go out.

✓ What to Verify

  • Any brand-new vehicle appears in Vehicles → Active tab with the correct stock number, VIN, year/make/model, and impound date.
  • If a vehicle existed in IM but wasn't in the CSV, it appears in the Possible Release section on Heather's dashboard with a red ghost banner.
  • No error messages or "0 new / 0 updated" result when you know there were new calls — if that happens, check that the file is the correct Towbook export format.
Right panel on Daily Intake: The right side is for BMV documents (LKA / title abstract PDFs). Drag those in there after the Towbook import — they attach to the matched vehicle automatically.
STEP 2 Find New Cars That Need a First Letter ▾

After importing, work through Heather's dashboard to see which vehicles need their first certified letter sent. The stoplight system color-codes urgency.

Heather Tim (view)
  • 1Click My Dashboard in the navbar (Heather), or Heather (Tim). This is Heather's main work screen.
  • 2Check the top of the dashboard first for any dark red blocking alerts: ghost vehicles (Possible Release) or No Record Found vehicles. These must be resolved before letters go out — the system will not let you print or send letters for flagged vehicles.
  • 3Review the RED — Overdue stoplight section. Each row shows: Stock #, Vehicle, Days Held, Active Task, and Action Needed. Any row showing "Send Letter 1 by [date]" where that date is in the past needs immediate action.
  • 4Review YELLOW — Due Soon. These letters are due within the next 7 days. Plan for these today so they don't become red tomorrow.
  • 5Check the BMV Search Queue (Task 1) below the stoplight. Vehicles here need a BMV title search done first — you need the owner and lienholder name/address before you can address a letter. Once you've completed the search, click "BMV Done" on that vehicle.
  • 6Alternate view: click Letters in the navbar → "Need to Send" sub-tab. This shows every unsent letter across all vehicles, sortable by days on lot.

✓ What to Verify

  • RED vehicles have a letter due date that is actually in the past.
  • GREEN vehicles genuinely have no action needed — no overdue letters, no missing BMV, not a ghost.
  • Ghost / Possible Release vehicles are not appearing in the letter queues — they should be in their own separate section with a red banner.
  • The "Recalculate" button (top right, Heather/Tim only) refreshes the urgency colors if something seems out of date.
STEP 3 Send the First Letter & Create a UPS Label ▾

Once a vehicle has owner/lienholder info from BMV, you print the letter, generate a UPS certified mail label in-system, and mark it sent. The system automatically schedules Letter 2 when you do this.

Heather Tim

Print the Letter

  • 1On the vehicle detail page, scroll to the Letters card. Click the letter type you need (e.g. "First Notice — Owner"). The letter opens in a new tab — print it from there.
  • 2Print the letter on your printer. Set it aside — you'll fold and envelope it after creating the UPS label.

Create UPS Label & Mark Sent

  • 3From Heather's dashboard or the Letters → "Need to Send" tab, click "Mark Sent" on the Letter 1 row for this vehicle.
  • 4On the Mark Sent page, click "Create UPS Label & Mark Sent" at the top. The system calls UPS and generates a certified mail label. The label image (GIF) appears on the page — this is what you print and stick on the envelope.
  • 5Print the label. It's ~4 inches wide, standard UPS label format. The label includes the owner name/address and the invoice number as the UPS reference number — this is how the system matches the envelope when it comes back.
  • 6Confirm the Date Sent field (defaults to today). The UPS tracking number auto-fills from the label that was just created.
  • 7For AFO (Accident for Owner) cases only: enter AFO in the Reference #2 field. This gets written on the physical label and will auto-flag the vehicle when the envelope comes back through the scanner.
  • 8Click Submit. The system records the sent date, tracking number, and automatically creates Letter 2 scheduled 30 days from today (the send date).
  • 9If the vehicle has a 2nd owner or 2nd lienholder (co-owner), a second UPS label is automatically created and shown below the first. Print both — they need separate envelopes.

✓ What to Verify

  • After submit: the vehicle detail page shows Letter 1 with a sent date + tracking number (should start with "1Z" — UPS format).
  • A Letter 2 row appears on the vehicle detail with a due date approximately 30 days from now.
  • Letter 2 shows up in Letters → Need to Send with "Second Notice" label and the future due date.
  • The vehicle moves from RED or YELLOW to GREEN on Heather's dashboard.
  • If a 2nd owner existed: two separate tracking numbers are shown on the vehicle detail — one for each recipient.
If UPS label creation fails: Enter the tracking number manually after creating the label on the UPS website. Use UPS Lookup in the navbar to search by invoice number and attach the tracking number later if needed.
STEP 4 Find Cars That Need a Second Letter ▾

Letter 2 becomes due 30 days after Letter 1 is SENT — the clock starts the day you send the 1st letter, not when it's delivered. So the moment Letter 1 goes out, the system already knows Letter 2's exact due date and will surface it on your board when it's time.

Heather Tim
What's automatic: the moment you mark Letter 1 sent in the app, the system schedules Letter 2 for exactly 30 days later and surfaces it on your dashboard when it's due — no clicking, no waiting on delivery. (UPS delivery is still tracked separately for your proof-of-delivery records, but it does NOT change when Letter 2 is due.) The steps below are just for looking up what's due; the timer runs itself.
  • 1Go to My Dashboard. In the RED or YELLOW sections, look for vehicles with "Send Letter 2 by [date]" in the Active Task column.
  • 2Or: click Letters → Need to Send. Second Notice rows are labeled "Second Notice — Owner" or "Second Notice — Lienholder."
  • 3Letter 2's due date is always exactly 30 days after Letter 1's sent date — you'll see both on the vehicle detail → Letters card. (Delivery status is shown too, for your records, but it does not change the due date.)

✓ What to Verify

  • Letter 2's due date is exactly 30 days after Letter 1's sent date — it does not wait on delivery.
  • The Audit tool (Tim/Brady/Jim) → Section 2 will also flag Letter 2 as overdue once 30+ days have passed since Letter 1 was sent.
  • Letter 2 rows show "Second Notice" in the label — not "First Notice."
STEP 5 Send the Second Letter ▾

The second letter flow is identical to the first. A brand-new UPS label is created (new tracking number, new shipment) — it does not reuse Letter 1's tracking.

Heather Tim
  • 1Print the second letter from the vehicle detail → Letters card → "Second Notice" link. It opens in a new tab with the "SECOND AND FINAL NOTICE" banner at the top.
  • 2Click "Mark Sent" on the Letter 2 row.
  • 3Click "Create UPS Label & Mark Sent" — same button, new label, new UPS tracking number.
  • 4Print the label, confirm date, submit.

✓ What to Verify

  • Vehicle detail shows Letter 2 with its own tracking number — different from Letter 1's.
  • For PPI impounds: no Letter 3 is created after Letter 2. The vehicle enters the waiting period before title eligibility.
  • For POLICE impounds: a 5-letter chain is used. Letters 3–6 appear at the right stages — check vehicle detail after marking Letter 2 sent.
STEP 6 Scan Returned Envelopes ▾

When UPS delivers an envelope or returns one, scanning it updates the letter record automatically. The AI reads the tracking number, delivery date, and outcome from the envelope image.

Heather

Method A — Single Envelope (webcam, one at a time)

  • 1Click Letters in the navbar. In the top-right corner, click "Scan Envelope." A camera modal opens.
  • 2Hold the envelope in front of the webcam so the tracking barcode, stamps, and delivery/return markings are clearly visible. Click Capture.
  • 3The AI reads the envelope and returns: tracking number, outcome (Delivered / Address Issue / Returned to Sender / Unknown), delivery date, and return reason.
  • 4A match confirmation card appears. Confirm it — this links the scan to the correct vehicle and updates the letter record. If no match is found, the scan is saved as Unmatched (not discarded).

Method B — Bulk Envelope Scan (50+ envelopes at once)

  • 5Scan all envelopes to image files (JPG or PNG) using a scanner or phone camera.
  • 6Click Photo Upload in the navbar → Bulk Envelope Scan. Select all envelope image files at once. The AI processes each one — matched vehicles update automatically; unmatched go to the Unmatched queue.

Method C — Envelopes Tab (review and manage all scans)

  • 7Click Envelopes in the navbar. Three tabs:
  • 8Matched tab: Scans linked to a vehicle. Click "Clear" once handled (Vehicle Sold / Vehicle Released / Manual). Cleared scans move to the archive tab.
  • 9Unmatched tab: Scans the AI couldn't auto-match. Click "Link to Vehicle" — search by stock number, plate, or owner name. Once linked, the letter record updates. Click "Discard" if it can't be identified.
  • 10Cleared tab: Archive of all handled scans with who cleared them and when.

✓ What to Verify

  • After scanning a delivered envelope: vehicle detail → Letters card shows a green checkmark and delivery confirmed date.
  • After scanning an RTS envelope: the letter shows a Return to Sender flag (red).
  • Unmatched scans appear in Envelopes → Unmatched tab — never silently dropped.
  • If unmatched scans exist, a yellow warning banner appears at the top of Heather's dashboard.
STEP 7 Refresh UPS & Check Which Letters Were Delivered ▾

Push UPS directly to get the latest tracking status on any sent letter — faster than waiting for a physical envelope to arrive.

Heather Tim

Option A — From Vehicle Detail Page

  • 1Open a vehicle detail page. Scroll to the Letters card.
  • 2For any sent letter with a tracking number that isn't yet confirmed delivered, click "Refresh from UPS."
  • 3The system queries UPS live. Status updates to: Label Created → In Transit → Out for Delivery → Delivered (or Return to Sender). A flash message shows the new status.
  • 4If delivered, the delivery date is recorded for your proof-of-delivery records. (Letter 2's due date is based on when Letter 1 was sent, not delivered — so this doesn't change the 2nd-letter timing.)
  • 5POD Image (Proof of Delivery): 7–10 days after delivery, a signed POD image becomes available on the vehicle detail. Click "Fetch POD" to retry manually if it hasn't appeared yet.

Option B — From Heather's Dashboard

  • 6On Heather's dashboard, scroll to "Awaiting Delivery Confirmation." Each row has a "Confirm Delivered" date form (for manual entry) and a "Scan" link to use the webcam.

Option C — UPS Lookup (when tracking number is missing)

  • 7Click UPS Lookup in the navbar (Heather only). Enter the invoice number or stock number. UPS returns all matching shipments.
  • 8Click "Attach" next to the shipment — this links the tracking number to the open letter so "Refresh from UPS" works going forward.

✓ What to Verify

  • After refresh: the letter status on the vehicle detail updates (e.g. "In Transit" or "Delivered").
  • Letter 2's due date is 30 days from Letter 1's sent date — delivery does not change it.
  • UPS Lookup finds shipments by invoice number and shows an Attach button on results matching an open untracked letter.
  • The POD image appears as a linked PDF on the vehicle detail once available (typically 7–10 business days after delivery).
RTS (Return to Sender): If UPS refresh detects a letter came back, it sets an RTS flag. Use the "Restart Letter Clock" button on the vehicle detail page after correcting the address.
End-to-End Test Checklist — Confirm the Site Is Working
1 · Import Test
Upload a Towbook CSV via Daily Intake. Confirm result shows correct new/updated/skipped counts. A new vehicle appears in Vehicles → Active with correct stock number and impound date.
2 · First Letter + UPS Label Test
Find a vehicle with no sent Letter 1. Click Mark Sent → Create UPS Label. Confirm: label appears with a 1Z tracking number. After submit: vehicle detail shows sent date + tracking, and Letter 2 is auto-scheduled ~30 days out.
3 · Dashboard Stoplight Test
Spot-check a RED vehicle — confirm its letter due date is actually in the past. Spot-check a GREEN vehicle — confirm it has a sent letter or no letters due. Click "Recalculate" and confirm colors don't change unexpectedly.
4 · Envelope Scan Test
Scan a real or test envelope image via the Scan Envelope camera. Confirm: AI reads tracking number and outcome. System proposes the correct vehicle match. After confirming: vehicle detail shows delivery confirmed date.
5 · Unmatched Envelope Test
Scan an envelope that doesn't match any active vehicle. Confirm: it appears in Envelopes → Unmatched tab (not silently lost). Heather's dashboard shows a yellow unmatched-scans warning banner.
6 · UPS Refresh Test
Click "Refresh from UPS" on a letter with a real tracking number. Confirm status updates. If delivered, delivery_confirmed_date is set (proof of delivery). Letter 2's due date does NOT change — it stays 30 days from Letter 1's sent date.
7 · Ghost Vehicle Test
Find a Possible Release vehicle. Confirm: it does NOT appear in letter queues. Confirm: clicking Mark Sent or Generate Letter for that vehicle shows a blocking error, not a form.
8 · Second Letter Timer Test
Check a vehicle with Letter 1 sent. Confirm: Letter 2 due date = ~30 days from Letter 1's sent date (not delivery). It shows a due date even if Letter 1 hasn't been delivered yet.
